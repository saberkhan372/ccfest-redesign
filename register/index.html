    04

    How to join on the day

    Virtual CC Fest runs on Zoom Events: one lobby, with several workshops happening at once in each round.

    1. Register via Luma. Sign up above to save your place.
    2. Watch for your Zoom invite. You will receive an invitation to the Zoom event the day before, with reminders in the hours before it starts.
    3. Join the lobby. On the day, open the Zoom event and join the lobby.
    4. Pick a session. There are two rounds of concurrent sessions, so drop in and out as you like. Questions go in the chat.

    Stuck? Email {{ site.data.site.email | escape }} and we’ll help you get in.
